# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a harassment-free research and discussion space for
everyone, regardless of body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the research community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, personal attacks,
publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would reasonably
be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Research Ethics

This project also adheres to responsible research practices:

- No active exploitation of systems you do not own or have permission to test.
- No publishing of working exploits or bypasses for the analyzed device.
- Respect for coordinated disclosure (see SECURITY.md).
- Data-minimization and privacy protection (see docs/DATENSCHUTZ.md).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers
via the contact methods in SECURITY.md. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
